# Sources and provenance

This package is an original Williams-inspired implementation. Its instructions
and examples were written for this repository; it does not vendor the existing
`poemswe/clarity-and-grace` package or reproduce a chapter from Williams.

The requested book is Joseph M. Williams, *Style: Toward Clarity and Grace*
(University of Chicago Press, 1990; two chapters coauthored with Gregory G.
Colomb). It is not the same edition/title as Williams and Joseph Bizup's *Style:
Lessons in Clarity and Grace*, which the existing skill cites. Bibliographic
verification: <https://lawcat.berkeley.edu/record/364937>.

Sources consulted on 2026-09-09:

- Boston University Writing Program, [Sentences Tell Stories: A Principle of
  Clarity](https://www.bu.edu/teaching-writing/resources/essential-lesson-10/).
  Its Williams-based curriculum grounds the diagnosis of participants, subjects,
  actions, and nominalizations, and discusses conflicts between principles.
- Pearson, [Style: The Basics of Clarity and Grace, fifth edition](https://www.pearson.com/en-nz/subject-catalog/p/style-the-basics-of-clarity-and-grace/P200000002141/9780321953308).
  The publisher characterizes the approach as reason-based guidance rather than
  inflexible rules and identifies its coverage of coherence, emphasis, shape,
  concision, and elegance. This is a different title, not evidence of a complete
  reading of the requested book.
- [poemswe/clarity-and-grace](https://github.com/poemswe/clarity-and-grace/tree/89a06d1d8d8d19b800ca26b5d8534994f554b5f0),
  pinned commit `89a06d1d8d8d19b800ca26b5d8534994f554b5f0`.
  The complete writing skill and all four companion files were inspected as
  the comparison target. Its Williams companion supplies additional coverage of
  familiar-to-new progression, topic continuity, and sentence-end emphasis.
  No license file was present in the inspected tree; no upstream text is bundled.

The priority on source fidelity, protected spans, task scope, uncertainty,
verification, and tool authorization is an agent-specific design choice, not a
claim that Williams supplied these exact instructions. This is not a scholarly
reconstruction of the entire book; a full-text edition-by-edition verification
has not been performed. The skill requires no runtime access to these sources.
