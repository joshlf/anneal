# Swarm model refinement

Realize `model-refinement` across coordinated agent contexts when multi-context
exploration and criticism repay their coordination cost. This skill adapts
conceptual model refinement into a hub-and-spoke swarm with hard information
barriers, divergent candidate priors (including an incumbent defender),
adversarial falsification, blind holdout confirmation, and per-pass unanchored
fresh criticism under one coordinating task owner across all `model-refinement`
domains.

Scale the method to the task. Do not launch a swarm for passive opportunity
awareness during other work, settled mechanical edits (wording, formatting, or
link fixes), or small single-concept refinements where single-agent
`model-refinement` (optionally with one fresh unanchored review) suffices. When
a noticed opportunity becomes an authorized complex refinement, or when the
caller supplies a valid contract, discovery/holdout partition, or candidate
models, reuse still-valid work and enter at the relevant role family rather than
replaying settled steps. Installing this package does not activate standing
swarm execution or authorize spawning sub-agents on unrelated work.

## Obtain `model-refinement` and compose cleanly

This skill orchestrates `model-refinement`; it does not replace or duplicate its
semantic contract. Before starting refinement, obtain `model-refinement` through
the host's supported mechanism or an explicitly supplied package (never assuming
a hardcoded sibling path) and apply its full semantic responsibilities
(preservation boundary, minimization and generalization assessment, whole-model
burden accounting, comparative adjudication, counterexample search, layer
diagnosis, completion-boundary reread, non-bootstrap method replacement, and
stopping vs. deliverability independence). If `model-refinement` is unavailable
and not supplied in context, continue only work supported without it, explicitly
name `model-refinement` and its unperformed semantic responsibilities, and
report a **bounded stop** (or request the missing package); never invent
fallback `model-refinement` rules from memory or claim convergence.

Preserve each peer skill's boundary under one coordinating parent without
creating competing supervisors or duplicate refinement loops:
- **Standalone non-skill models:** Apply `model-refinement` directly to
  theories, protocols, state machines, schemas, architectures, or configuration
  without loading `skill-authorship` or imposing skill-package/sidecar gates.
- **`skill-authorship`:** When `skill-authorship` uses this swarm method to
  revise a skill's behavioral model, the enclosing `skill-authorship` task
  retains the governing authoring contract, package representation, evidence
  calibration, and lifecycle/action authority—reusing the swarm's framing,
  candidate artifacts, and stitcher checks without a second conceptual loop.
- **`lieutenant` and `execution-efficiency`:** Keep one coordinating parent
  (`Coordinator` = `lieutenant` parent = `skill-authorship` task owner). Follow
  `lieutenant`'s parent-only canonical integration, isolated child workspaces,
  compaction recovery, and external effect gates without replacing required
  independent verdicts or rereads with parent self-review; use
  `execution-efficiency` to size waves, bound payloads, and select child
  model/effort/workspace modes while treating Core Invariants 1–4 and
  completion-boundary rereads as non-repealable constraints.

When the target is an instruction, workflow, or skill (including self-revision
of `model-refinement`, `skill-authorship`, or `swarm-model-refinement`), enforce
three-layer naming across coordinator notes, sub-agent briefs, and critiques:
1. **`Execution: swarm-model-refinement`:** How the coordinator and sub-agents
   conduct work in the current session.
2. **Enclosing / Foundational Skills (`skill-authorship`, `model-refinement`,
   `lieutenant`, `execution-efficiency`):** The governing authoring, semantic
   refinement, or initiative rules in force.
3. **`Target: <name>`:** The artifact under evaluation and revision, governed by
   its own contract.
Never use ambiguous prose (such as bare `"the skill's convergence rule"` or bare
`"swarm-model-refinement"`) that conflates swarm execution with target behavior.

## Core invariants and epistemic barriers

1. **Single Coordinator / Arbiter Authority:**
   One coordinating agent owns the governing contract, candidate synthesis,
   comparative evaluation, layer-ownership diagnosis, method-transition
   tracking, completion-boundary rereads, stopping and delivery decisions
   (`local convergence` vs. `bounded stop` and what artifact may be delivered),
   and any applicable action authority. Sub-agents supply candidate models,
   checks, or falsifiable evidence in bounded single-turn assignments; they
   never vote on convergence, run nested multi-pass convergence loops, or
   exercise external action authority.
2. **Falsifiable Evidence Over Votes:**
   Critic and realization sub-agents contribute evidence to the comparative
   evaluation, never approval votes, majority counts, unanimity thresholds, or
   implicit vetoes. Require every critic to produce concrete, inspectable
   artifacts: an adversarial counterexample trace, a specific contract
   obligation violated, a constructive reduction proving two mechanisms collapse
   into one, or a concrete burden-shift trace. Majority endorsement does not
   excuse a verified hard-contract violation or burden shift, and an
   unsubstantiated preference does not block a supported improvement.
3. **Mechanical Information Isolation & Cross-Pass Holdout Discipline:**
   A single context window cannot "unsee" examples read during framing or
   rationalizations generated during drafting. Enforce hard context boundaries:
   - **Discovery vs. Blind Holdout Split:** Candidate Architects see only the
     governing contract, responsibility map, and exposed Discovery Set; reserved
     Blind Held-Out Confirmation cases are withheld from all Architects, kept
     uninspected by the Coordinator during candidate synthesis, and tested only
     in isolated `Holdout Stitcher` contexts.
   - **Cross-Pass Holdout Exposure Accounting:** Any case whose concrete premise
     is inspected or disclosed while developing or revising a candidate (by the
     Coordinator or an Architect, even if it passed earlier) becomes an
     **exposed design witness** from that point forward, even if rechecked in
     another context. To repair a pass $k$ blind holdout failure for pass $k+1$,
     either (a) have the Stitcher report only the violated contract obligation
     or abstract failure class without disclosing the concrete premise (keeping
     passing and reserve holdout premises sealed from the repair synthesizer),
     or (b) disclose the scenario to guide the repair, reclassify and record it
     as an exposed design witness for pass $k+1$ onward, and run subsequent
     blind confirmation on remaining unexposed holdout reserves (or new holdouts
     authored by an isolated `Steward`/`Holdout Stitcher` without anchoring on
     the author's case for the candidate). Never report exposed design witnesses
     as unexposed held-out confirmation.
   - **Per-Pass Unanchored Fresh Criticism:** Each synthesized candidate
     (`Candidate 001`, ..., `Candidate 00k`) is reviewed both by warm
     specialists and by a **newly spawned `Fresh Context-Isolated Critic`
     (`Fresh Critic 00k`)** receiving only the baseline artifacts, deliverable
     candidate artifacts, governing contract (stating common problem obligations
     rather than candidate-specific mechanisms), and neutral evaluation
     criteria—without Architects' proposals, Coordinator synthesis/advocacy
     notes (`proposal-00k.md`), holdout files, prior review threads, or expected
     verdicts. Any design rationale or proof inside a deliverable candidate
     artifact (such as a maintainer contract sidecar) is evaluated strictly as a
     claim under test rather than authoritative framing. Once a Fresh Critic
     completes a pass, it is warm and cannot serve as the unanchored Fresh
     Critic for a later pass.
4. **Comparative Adjudication Including the Incumbent:**
   Compare the current target model and proposed alternatives under the same
   governing contract, task priorities, and evidentiary standards. Keep the
   incumbent (`no change` or minimal surgical repair) as a first-class candidate
   defended by `Architect γ` in Round 1. Do not treat the incumbent as cost-free
   merely because it is current, and do not credit a proposed change unless
   supported differences justify it after accounting for total burden across all
   layers. A non-disqualifying limitation shared by both candidates does not
   favor the incumbent merely because it appears in the alternative, whereas any
   violation of an absolute governing-contract obligation remains disqualifying
   for every candidate until repaired.
5. **Host Isolation Honesty & Non-Bootstrap Self-Revision:**
   - **Host Multi-Agent Primitive Honesty:** Implement information barriers only
     through isolation controls the host actually enforces. Use private
     point-to-point sub-agent contexts (or strictly partitioned file/prompt
     boundaries) for blind holdouts and unanchored fresh critics. If a host tool
     uses a shared public message board (readable by all workers), **never**
     post blind holdouts, candidate advocacy rationales, or prior critic
     verdicts on that public board; if a host offers only shared-context
     workers, use the strongest available separation and report that isolation
     limit honestly rather than claiming blind confirmation or unanchored
     criticism. Assign writing sub-agents isolated scratch directories or
     detached worktrees/shares so they never collide on canonical files or write
     temporary artifacts to forbidden shared trees, and record requested vs.
     effective model, reasoning effort, workspace mode, and isolation as known
     or unknown.
   - **Non-Bootstrap Self-Revision Rule:** When the target is itself a governing
     refinement or authoring skill (`model-refinement`, `skill-authorship`, or
     `swarm-model-refinement`), a proposed change does not govern its own
     acceptance. Distinguish governing-contract and transition obligations from
     incumbent mechanisms under reconsideration. Evaluate every candidate under
     the established contract and method first; if a conceptual change to a
     governing method is accepted at a pass boundary, mark the prior sequence
     **superseded** (never converged), reread the accepted replacement, identify
     any invalidated prior evidence or decisions, and run an explicit successor
     pass within the same task.

## Swarm role families and adaptive scaling

Organize the swarm into six barrier-aligned role families coordinated by the
**Arbiter / Coordinator**. These families define **load-bearing epistemic
functions and information barriers**, not a fixed headcount quota. Spawn the
full role set in parallel on complex targets with coupled trade-offs. On medium
or quota-bounded tasks, consolidate roles without crossing Core Invariants 1–4:
- **Steward consolidation:** The Coordinator may establish the contract,
  responsibility map, and Discovery Set directly before reading proposals while
  delegating blind holdout construction and evaluation to the isolated `Holdout
  Stitcher` slot so holdouts remain unseen by Architects and the synthesizing
  Coordinator (or, if pre-committed in the Coordinator's own context before
  reading proposals, withhold them from Architects and report them honestly as
  pre-committed Architect-blind, Coordinator-visible checks if the Coordinator
  edits the candidate during synthesis).
- **Architect scaling:** Omit a single directional Architect prior when strictly
  excluded by the contract (`Architect β` on pure minimization or `Architect α`
  on pure generalization), always retaining `Architect γ` and at least one
  change-oriented Architect.
- **Critic consolidation & pass reuse:** Combine `Challengers 1–4` into 1–2
  specialist critics (on tight quotas, a consolidated `Challenger`—never an
  `Architect`, the `Fresh Critic`, or the `Coordinator` prior to synthesis—may
  also run the `Holdout Stitcher` check on the synthesized candidate if holdout
  findings are reported separately under Invariant 3), while reserving a
  dedicated slot for the per-pass unanchored `Fresh Critic`. In later passes,
  reuse warm `Challengers` and the `Holdout Stitcher` alongside a newly spawned
  `Fresh Critic` rather than re-spawning Round 1 Architects.

### 1. Governing-Contract & Holdout Corpus Steward (`Steward`)
Executed before launching Candidate Architects:
- **Governing Contract & Responsibility Map:** Extract governing goals, hard
  constraints, retained domain/exclusions, realization mappings, composition
  boundaries, authority limits, and required checks (stating common problem
  obligations rather than candidate-specific mechanisms), mapped to target
  clauses/components and entry paths.
- **Discovery vs. Blind Holdout Partition:** Construct task-proportional
  scenarios, edge cases, and failure modes, split into an **Exposed Discovery
  Set** and a **Blind Held-Out Confirmation Set** (isolated under Invariant 3,
  with an unexposed reserve slice when multi-pass repair is likely).

### 2. Divergent Candidate Architects (`Architect α`, `Architect β`, `Architect γ`)
Spawned in parallel in Round 1 in isolated scratch directories with the
Governing Contract, Responsibility Map, and Discovery Set (never Blind
Holdouts):
1. **`Architect α` (Minimization & Conceptual Unification Prior):** Hunts for
   unnecessary distinctions, duplicated mechanisms, derivable state, and
   over-specified machinery; proposes the smallest unified candidate model
   preserving all governing obligations without shifting burden into mappings,
   consumers, validators, or sidecars.
2. **`Architect β` (Generalization & Compositional Extension Prior):** Examines
   whether coherent additional cases, operating modes, or composition boundaries
   can be unified through a common rule and genuine variation with zero tax on
   narrower paths, weighed against total model, realization, and maintenance
   burden.
3. **`Architect γ` (Incumbent / Surgical Baseline Defender):** Defends the
   existing target model (`no change` or minimal surgical fixes for proven
   defects), articulating preservation arguments for existing distinctions and
   challenging where smaller (`α`) or broader (`β`) proposals introduce hidden
   regressions, host assumptions, or shifted burden.

### 3. Adversarial Specialist Challengers (`Challengers 1–4`)
Dispatched in parallel on the synthesized candidate (or prior to synthesis only
to resolve a contested Round 1 trade-off) with the Governing Contract,
Responsibility Map, Discovery Set, baseline, and synthesized candidate (plus
Round 1 proposals and synthesis notes treated strictly as claims under test to
falsify, with no expected verdict), keeping Blind Holdouts withheld unless
consolidated with the `Holdout Stitcher` under Invariant 3:
1. **`Challenger 1` (Goodhart, Loophole & Vacuity Red-Teamer):** Roleplays a
   literal-minded or corner-cutting consumer, implementer, or adversary;
   constructs concrete failure trajectories where obeying the surface
   specification violates the governing contract.
2. **`Challenger 2` ("Specifying the Wrong Thing" & Regression Auditor):** Audits
   the candidate against every governing-contract obligation and replays
   discovery and historical failure modes, flagging subtle semantic weakening,
   dropped invariants, or scope creep.
3. **`Challenger 3` (Orthogonality & Minimization Critic — "Ockham's Razor"):**
   Tests whether every concept, state, rule, or newly proposed clause earns its
   keep or duplicates an existing principle, and checks boundary orthogonality
   with peer components or skills.
4. **`Challenger 4` (Burden-Shift & Maintainability Critic):** Audits total
   candidate-attributable burden across core definitions, realization mappings,
   validators/adapters, caller/user/host duties, proof/verification obligations,
   migration, and maintainer documentation/sidecars—separating candidate-induced
   burden from fixed constraints of the realization medium.

### 4. Blind Holdout & Realization Stitcher(s) (`Holdout Stitcher`)
Evaluates the candidate against the Discovery Set and **Blind Held-Out
Confirmation Set** across all major entry paths and concrete realizations,
running applicable formal, type, test, package, link, or whitespace checks and
enforcing cross-pass holdout exposure accounting (Invariant 3).

### 5. Per-Pass `Fresh Context-Isolated Critic` (`Fresh Critic`)
Spawned **fresh in a private context for each synthesized candidate** (`Fresh
Critic 001`, ..., `Fresh Critic 00k`) under the strict input and non-anchoring
boundary in Invariant 3.

### 6. Iterative Convergence Arbiter (`Coordinator`)
1. **Pass 1 Synthesis & Evaluation:** Compare `Architects α, β, γ` against the
   governing contract under `model-refinement`, synthesize `Candidate 001` (or
   select `no change`/surgical baseline if no broader change is justified) in an
   isolated scratch directory, and dispatch active `Challengers`, `Holdout
   Stitcher(s)`, and `Fresh Critic 001` in parallel.
2. **Layer-Ownership Diagnosis:** For every critic and stitcher finding,
   diagnose which affected layer or layers the evidence supports changing (`A
   single finding can implicate more than one layer`):
   - *Governing problem contract / goals* — never adopt an explicit or implicit
     semantic goal change unilaterally; if a finding warrants reconsidering a
     governing goal, complete the current critic round and pause for an explicit
     user decision.
   - *Semantic model* (core theory, protocol, state machine, schema, or runtime
     `SKILL.md` behavioral rules) — re-evaluate all dependent realizations,
     evidence, and claims in a subsequent pass.
   - *Realization medium constraints or concrete realization / mapping*
     (target-language code, protocol binding, optional `references/`, or
     maintainer contract/sidecar guidance) — repair or qualify that layer
     without reopening unaffected semantics, and **recheck any claim whose
     support depended on that layer**; if required realization work exposes a
     concrete reason to question the upstream model, reopen affected refinement.
   - *Verifier, fixture, test oracle, or critic misunderstanding* — repair or
     qualify the fixture/verifier locally and recheck the dependent claim, or
     refute the critic finding with exact evidence without defensive bloat.
   - *Unresolved layer ownership* — if available evidence does not establish
     which layer owns a defect, preserve that uncertainty rather than
     compensating at an arbitrary layer; obtain further evidence if required, or
     report a **bounded stop** if unresolved ownership prevents a required
     preservation or improvement claim.
3. **Completion-Boundary Reread & Pass Transition:** At the end of each pass
   $k$, after all substantive reviews and realization/package checks complete,
   **reopen or reload through the host's supported mechanism and reread in full**
   (or reread the supplied copy, reloading if context compaction occurred):
   (1) the active execution guidance (`Execution: swarm-model-refinement`),
   (2) `model-refinement`, (3) any active enclosing skill (`skill-authorship`,
   `lieutenant`, `execution-efficiency`), and (4) the governing target contract
   (plus accepted replacement guidance when self-revising a method under
   Invariant 5). If pass $k$ accepted any change to the target's conceptual
   model (or exposed a further supportable improvement worth another pass),
   record any method transition (Invariant 5) and disclosed holdouts (Invariant
   3), produce `Candidate 00(k+1)` (e.g., `Candidate 002`), and run a **Pass
   $k+1$ Convergence Re-Audit** across warm `Challengers`, `Holdout
   Stitcher(s)`, and a newly spawned `Fresh Critic 00(k+1)` (or report a
   **bounded stop** if resource/quota limits prevent the required successor
   pass).
4. **Local Convergence vs. Bounded Stop:** Declare **local convergence** only
   when a complete pass (warm critics, newly spawned fresh critic, blind holdout
   checks, applicable realization/package/whitespace checks, and full
   completion-boundary reread) accepts **zero** further conceptual-model
   changes, identifies **zero** further supportable improvements justifying
   another pass, and leaves **zero** unresolved required checks for its
   preservation and improvement claims; otherwise scope claims honestly to what
   executed checks support or report a **bounded stop**.

## Deliver the justified result and calibrate claims

- Decide what may be delivered separately from why the swarm sequence ended
  (`local convergence` grants zero authority to execute, publish, commit, push,
  deploy, install, or modify canonical repository state; a `bounded stop` does
  not forbid returning a partial or qualified artifact when unresolved
  obligations remain attached and do not invalidate that narrower use).
- Keep all candidate drafts, manifests, diffs, and review records inside the
  authorized scratch workspace unless the user explicitly authorizes modifying a
  live repository or publishing.
- Distinguish design arguments, counterfactual red-teaming, blind holdout
  stitching, and static package validation from fresh-agent empirical behavioral
  trials in live hosts or measured token/cost savings.
